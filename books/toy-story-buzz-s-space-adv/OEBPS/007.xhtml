As the space rangers began to explore the rocky gray planet, they
heard a strange humming sound.

Buzz and his crew looked over the edge of a huge canyon and saw
hundreds of Zurgbots— soldiers in Emperor Zurg’s army.

The Zurgbots spotted the space rangers and rushed toward them.

“We’ve got this covered, Captain!” cried Woody. While he and Rex
fought off the Zurgbots, Buzz went on in search of Zurg.
