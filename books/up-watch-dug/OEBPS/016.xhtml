Dug snuck across the clearing. He spotted fresh paw prints in the dirt.
 Then he saw a shadow near the picnic basket. “Woof, woof, woof!” he
 barked.

The intruder turned toward him. It had a black mask and striped tail.
 It was a raccoon, and it was stealing food from the picnic basket! “Move
 away from the basket!” Dug said. “That is our breakfast! Not yours!”
