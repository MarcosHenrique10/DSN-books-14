Dug sat up straight and looked at the moon . . . then
 suddenly a bat swooped down over the clearing and Dug
 jumped in surprise. Splash! He accidentally knocked over the
 jug of lemonade and spilled it all over the ground.

Dug slunk over to sit by Carl’s tent, where it wasn’t as
 wet—and that’s when he heard the sound. Scratch, scratch, scratch. What was that?!
