Dug bounded over some rocks, scrambled up a hill,
 and crossed a little stream. The dog was looking for a spot
 to camp for the night.

Following Dug were his friends Carl Fredricksen and a
 Junior Wilderness Explorer named Russell. Dug had met
 them in South America when Carl and Russell were on
 an adventure. Dug wanted to find the best camping spot
 ever—just for them!
