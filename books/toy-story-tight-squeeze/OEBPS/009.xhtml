“Eighteen . . . nineteen . . . twenty . . .” the toys counted.

Hamm was running out of time! He ran to Andy’s lunch box
 and hopped inside. “That was close,” he sighed as he shut
 the lid. But it was even darker in there than under Andy’s bed!

I wonder how long it’ll take for someone to find me,
 thought Hamm. But it didn’t take long at all. A minute later,
 a Green Army Man lifted the lid and rappelled in with his
 whole battalion.
