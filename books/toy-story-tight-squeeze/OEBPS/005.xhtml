“Okay,” Woody called to all the toys in
 Andy’s room. “The coast is clear.”

It was early morning and Andy had just left
 for school. The toys had the room to themselves
 for the whole day.

Woody was excited to play a game called
 “sardines.”

“It’s like hide-and-seek,” he said, “except
 when you're ‘It,’ you hide and everyone tries to
 find you. Then, when someone does, you both
 hide, and so on!”

“So, by the end of the game, everyone is
 hiding together in one spot?” Jessie asked,
 confused.

Woody nodded. “Right, except for the last
 toy left. In the next game, that toy is ‘It’—the
 one who hides!”
