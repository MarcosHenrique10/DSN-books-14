Things only got worse as more and more
 toys found the hiding place.

“Ow, your paw is in my ear!” a Green Army
 Man told Slinky Dog.

Buzz heard the toys complaining, and that’s
 when he appeared.

By the time Rex found the hiders, there was
 no way he could fit.

“No fair!” he exclaimed. “I found you guys,
 but there’s no room for me to hide here. What
 do I do now?”

“Shhh! Everyone will know where we’re
 hiding,” Woody warned. But it was too late.
 The rest of the toys were already hurrying over
 to the overstuffed lunch box.
