“Oh, well,” Woody laughed. “Everyone’s found us, so this
 game’s over. Everybody out!”

One by one, the toys tumbled out of the lunch box.

“Couldn’t you have picked a bigger place, Hamm?”
 Rex asked.

Hamm’s cheeks blushed red as the toys waited for his
 reply. “Well, yeah. But isn’t the point of the game to get
 squished? Like sardines in a can?” he said.

 The toys thought that over.

“You’ve got a point there, Hamm,” said Buzz.

And from then on, every time the toys played sardines,
 the hider made sure to pick a small hiding place—just to
 keep things interesting!
