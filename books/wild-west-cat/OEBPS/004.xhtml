A long time ago in the old Wild West, there was a cat
named Callie. She was the bravest, bestest cowgirl
who ever rode the range.

On her horse, named Sparky, Callie could gallop so fast
that sparks flew behind them like shooting stars.

And with her magic noodle lasso,
she could rope a twister and ride it like a
bronco in a rodeo ring.
