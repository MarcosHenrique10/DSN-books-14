When Jessie spotted a box
of old toys in Andy’s mom’s
car, she knew just what to do.
The box was being donated to
Sunnyside, the local daycare
center. There’d be plenty of
kids to play with there!

Woody followed the other toys
into the box. He tried to convince
them to stay, but he was too late.
Andy’s mom got into her car and
drove away. The toys were going
to Sunnyside!
