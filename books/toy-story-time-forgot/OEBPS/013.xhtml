“Strangers,” he calls. “From the unforgiving Outlands.”

Trixie continues to play her role, saying, “We have traveled far—from the distant
Bonnie tribe!”

Buzz and Woody hold back their laughter, but the Cleric just wants them gone.

To Trixie’s delight, Reptillus asks if they can stay. “Are dinosaurs not welcome in our
great city, wise Cleric?” he asks.

“Fine, fine,” the Cleric says. “Have them made presentable. I find their lack of armor
disturbing.”

While Reptillus escorts Trixie and Rex to the armory, Woody and Buzz are silently
captured by the Battlesaur brutes!

The Cleric laughs and says, “Welcome to Battleopolis.”
