“That sure was a fun Christmas!” Bonnie cheers
as she picks up her Santa Woody doll from beneath
the Christmas tree. She’s having a great time with her
toys and suddenly calls out, “Hey, there, Baby
Reindeer!”

But it’s not a reindeer . . . it’s Trixie, the

triceratops, with pipe cleaners for antlers. Right now
she’s playing a baby reindeer, but she also plays a goblin
fairy, a ghost clown, and even Mr. Potato Head’s mom!
What will she play next?

“Oh, no! My dinosaur has escaped!”

Bonnie cries.

Now Trixie is thrilled. “Did you hear that, Rex? For
once, I’m finally going to get played with as a dinosaur!”

Instead, Bonnie reaches right past Trixie and chooses
Angel Kitty to play the part of the dinosaur—or
Kittysaurus, in this case.

Trixie is disappointed. Luckily, Bonnie has a playdate
at her friend Mason’s house later, and Trixie, Woody,
Buzz, Rex, and Angel Kitty are all going along. Maybe
Trixie will finally get her chance to play a real
dinosaur!
