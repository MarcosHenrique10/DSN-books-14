Trixie has had enough. She attacks the Goliathon, trying to save her friends. When she
crashes into its hard exterior, she falls and the Bonnie mark on her foot is revealed.

“As I suspected,” the Cleric announces. “She bears the mark of obedience!”

“I . . . I thought I knew you,” Reptillus says, feeling betrayed.

“I was thinking the same thing,” Trixie replies, upset and disappointed by her friend.

“She is an enemy to the tribe,” the Cleric tells the crowd. He wants to
dishonor Trixie so that this talk of play will cease.
