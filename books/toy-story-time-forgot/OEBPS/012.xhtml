Reptillus Maximus, a dynamic Battlesaur warrior, appears from above. He somersaults

down from his Volcano Command Base to question the outlanders.

Buzz encourages Woody to talk to the heroic Battlesaur, but Reptillus is only
interested in Rex and Trixie.

“I am Reptillus Maximus!” he says. “What fearsome dinosaurs are these?”

Trixie and Rex share a pleased look.

Trixie, excited and nervous, answers, “I am . . . um . . . my name’s Trixie, it’s Trixie.”

As she and Reptillus become acquainted, a horn sounds to announce the arrival
of the Cleric.
