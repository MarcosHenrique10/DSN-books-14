Meanwhile, back in Battleopolis, the
Cleric is still controlling Rex! Rex’s
mech-arms reach inside the Goliathon’s
mouth and pull out Buzz, Woody . . .
and even Angel Kitty! Once out of the
Goliathon, they notice that the Cleric can
see Bonnie and Mason through the scope in
the Triassic Tower.

“Oh, I get it,” says Buzz. “You’re glad
Mason got the Optimum X.”

With Mason distracted by the video
game, he doesn’t have time to play with his
new toys. And that’s just how the Cleric
likes it. “He plays there while
I rule here. My Battlesaurs have no
use for play,” he tells Buzz and Woody.

To keep Bonnie’s toys quiet so he can
continue to rule, the Cleric decides to throw
Buzz, Woody, and Angel Kitty into the pit.
“The pit” turns out to be a floor vent with
rapidly spinning fan blades!
