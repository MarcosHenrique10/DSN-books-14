Suddenly, Woody and Buzz are
shoved up onto the stage.

“Trixie!” Woody calls. “The Battlesaurs

are not playing. They’ve never been
played with! They don’t even
know they’re toys!”

The Cleric cackles and draws
his finger across his throat. The
Battlesaurs in the stadium cheer.

Reptillus winds up and runs toward
Woody and Buzz. They try to fight
him off using a crayon and Buzz’s
spring-loaded wings, but they are no
match for Reptillus. Trixie needs to
step in to defend her friends.

“Back off! You . . . you bully!” she

cries. She hooks Reptillus with her horns
and flips him backward through the air.
