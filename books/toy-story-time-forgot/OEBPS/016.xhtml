On their walk back to the command base, Reptillus asks about Trixie’s home.

“Oh, it’s nothing like this,” she says. “I’ll bet you have the most amazing playtimes.”

“Play. Times,” Reptillus responds, confused. “I . . . do not understand.”

“You know, play. When you give yourself over to a
kid,” she explains.

He reacts quickly, “Giving is surrender. A
Battlesaur would never surrender.”

Trixie can’t help laughing at Reptillus’s seriousness,
but she continues to play along.

Finally, Reptillus looks at her and says, “Trixie, you
cause me to question all that I know.”
