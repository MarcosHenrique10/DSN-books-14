The Cleric lurks behind them in the shadows, not liking the direction the conversation
is taking. The last thing he wants is for Reptillus to question the Battlesaur code.

So he strikes his staff on the ground, which triggers the voice of the Triassic Tower.
“Time for battle!”

Battlesaurs fill the stands in the Arena of Woe, and Reptillus and Trixie both
bound across the stage and roar like fierce dinosaurs.

The Cleric wheels himself to a throne overlooking the arena and commands that the battle begin.
