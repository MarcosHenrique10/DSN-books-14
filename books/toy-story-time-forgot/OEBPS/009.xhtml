As Bonnie’s toys emerge from her
backpack, they look around the room and
quickly notice that something isn’t quite right.

“Where is everybody? Where’s
Sock Ape and the little wrestlers?” Trixie asks.

“We’ve got all afternoon to find our old
friends,” Woody reminds her.

“Or maybe some new ones!” Trixie says,
eyeing a pile of just-opened Christmas gifts.

“‘Battlesaurs: The Ultimate Dinosaurs,’” she reads off a box.
“I love it.”

Then she sees a little toy that looks like
a cross between a dinosaur and a dog. The
dino-hound runs away from Bonnie’s toys,
so Trixie decides to follow him!

“Come on, gang! Let’s stay together!”
Woody calls to the rest of the toys as he
chases Trixie through the playroom.
