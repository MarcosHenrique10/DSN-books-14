Just in time, too! Bonnie and Mason
run through the playroom gathering up their
toys. Woody, Buzz, and Angel Kitty are
saved from the pit!

With all the toys together, Mason and
Bonnie pretend that everyone is having a
dance party—not quite what the

Cleric was imagining.

Reptillus loves it, though, and even tells
Trixie how glorious it is to be played with.

When Bonnie heads home a little while
later, Reptillus is sad to see his new friend

Trixie go. But she’ll be back next week for
another playdate.
