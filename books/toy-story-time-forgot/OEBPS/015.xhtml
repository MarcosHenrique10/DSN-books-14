Rex and Trixie make their way to the armory with the
Cleric and Reptillus. When they get there, they meet
Ray-Gon, the scientist who runs the armory.

Trixie admires the vast array of battle gear he’s
collected, and together they work on the perfect
armor for her. Soon, she struts around, loving her new
role of Battlesaur.

But they can’t leave before Rex gets his turn! “Ready
for me?” he asks excitedly.

Just before Rex gets his armor on, the Cleric whispers
a command to Ray-Gon. Moments later, Rex has huge

Battlesaur mech-arms in place of his T. rex arms.

Trixie and Rex strike a pose together.

“Who’s a fierce dinosaur now?” Rex asks proudly.

“The ultimate dinosaurs!”
Trixie agrees.
