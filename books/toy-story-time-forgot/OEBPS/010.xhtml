When Woody, Buzz, Rex, and Angel
Kitty finally catch up to Trixie, they
find her standing in front of a huge
prehistoric-city play set. It looks
like it’s built from giant dinosaur bones
and lava rocks. Trixie is in awe!

Suddenly, secret panels swing open
and a horde of raptor guards charge
out from all directions.

“They’re like dinosaurs
plus!” Trixie cheers.

The leader steps forward and says,
“Speak swiftly, foolish Outlanders,
or suffer my Battle Blade.”

Trixie, thinking that she is playing
dinosaurs with this new group of toys,
replies, “We are savage warriors,
eager to slake our endless thirst for
conquest.”

The guards seem impressed and
lower their toy weapons.
