Fortunately, Trixie makes it to the media
room, where Bonnie and Mason are still
playing the video game. She decides that
she has to shut the game down!
She travels over the wires and power strips,
losing her armor on the way. She is almost
there when Reptillus stops her from turning
the game off.

“Reptillus! Your world is bigger than you
know! Let me show you who you really are!”
she pleads.

“But . . . I am a Battlesaur!”

he responds, less sure than he’s ever been.

“But it’s your kid who chooses what
you’re going to be,” Trixie explains. “It
could be a dinosaur . . . a baby reindeer . . .
or something you’d never even think of.
It’s about being there for your kid. It’s . . .
it’s about . . .”

“Surrender,” Reptillus finishes.
And with that, he shuts down the game.
