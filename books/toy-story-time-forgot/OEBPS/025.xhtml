The Cleric snaps his fingers at Ray-Gon, who brings him the controller from the
Armory. With this, the Cleric has full control over Rex and his mech-arms! Rex is helpless
to resist as the Cleric forces him to advance on Trixie!

“There is no escape, Trixie of the Bonn-Ye tribe,” the Cleric says, laughing.

But Trixie is determined. There is only one way to save her friends: she needs to
find Bonnie! She dodges Rex, a pack of dino-hounds, the raptor guards, and the
Battlesaur brutes and races through the playroom.

Reptillus follows closely behind, so Trixie leads him in front of his original packaging. If she

can show him that he is a toy, maybe he’ll believe that he’s meant to be played with.

When he sees his toy box, he’s upset and confused. Should he believe Trixie or the Cleric?
