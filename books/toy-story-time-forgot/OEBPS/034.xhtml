Back at Bonnie’s house, the other toys greet Woody, Buzz, Rex, Angel Kitty, and Trixie.

“So, how was the playdate?” Jessie asks.

“Oh, typical crash-landing . . .” Woody replies.

“. . . hostile lizard people, hand-to-hand combat . . .” Buzz continues.

“. . . and I was a mindless, lumbering automaton!” Rex finishes.

“What about you, Trixie?” Mr. Pricklepants asks. “Have you finally found a role that
suits you?”

“Goblin fairy, ghost clown, dancing queen, a baby reindeer—you name it!” Trixie cheers.

“I’m Bonnie’s dinosaur. And Bonnie’s dinosaur gets to be everything!”
