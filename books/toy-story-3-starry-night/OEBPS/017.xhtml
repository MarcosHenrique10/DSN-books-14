“Look! The stars are out!” said Buttercup as
 soon as they got outside.

Buzz smiled and pointed to a constellation.
 “That, my friend, is the Big Dipper—seven stars
 that form a ladle shape,” he told his pals.

Mr. Potato head looked up. “I just saw a
 shooting star!” he cried.

Hamm looked up, too. “I think that was a
 firefly,” he replied.
