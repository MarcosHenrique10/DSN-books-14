Vee’s doorbell shrieks again. “Our monster guests must be here!” she says.

Soon the Hauntleys’ house is full of ghouls. There are mummies, goblins, witches,
 and even Vee’s favorite band from Transylvania, the Scream Girls!

“You came all the way here for my party?” Vee squeals.

“We wouldn’t miss it!” Franken-Stacey says.
