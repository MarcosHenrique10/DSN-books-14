Vee sighs with relief as she watches Edgar open the
 valentine he was supposed to get in the first place.

Goblins are green.
Vampires are blue.
You’re a spooktacular friend,
Happy Valentine’s
Day to you!

“Thanks, Vee,” Edgar says. “It’s spooky and awesome! If more
 valentines were like this, I wouldn’t mind Valentine’s Day so much!”

Vee can’t believe it. Edgar likes her valentine!
