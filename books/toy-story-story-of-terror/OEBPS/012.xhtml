Terrified, Jessie huddled in the bathroom. Suddenly, someone pulled her
beneath the sink.

“Quiet, soldier. You’ll lead them right to us!” said an army toy missing one
hand. He had been living the life of a fugitive. “Combat Carl’s seen things,
horrible things,” he continued.

“Are you Combat Carl?” asked Jessie. She didn’t see anyone else around.

Just then, growling came from above. “We’ve got to move,” said Combat Carl.

“What? No!” said Jessie. As she tried to catch up, Combat Carl was
dragged away by a mysterious creature!
