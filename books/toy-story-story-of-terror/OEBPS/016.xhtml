The toys were trying to unlock
the cabinet door when the office
computer suddenly chimed.
Woody had been sold!

The manager rushed into the
office, took Woody out of the
cabinet, and packed him in a box.

 

“What started out as a classic horror film has turned
into something more of a tragedy,” said Mr. Pricklepants.

A moment later, the computer chimed again. This time,
Jessie had been sold!
