The toys began to search for Mr. Potato Head,
and suddenly—

“Ew! I stepped in something!” yelled Rex.

Buzz dashed over to taste the slime.
“Disgusting,” he said.

Meanwhile, Trixie had followed a slime trail to
a vent—and she fell in!

As the toys peered in after her, Mr. Pricklepants
spoke up. “This would be the threshold. Every
movie has one. Once the heroes enter, there’s no
turning back.”

Before the gang could lower themselves into
the vent, a scared Rex came running and knocked
them all in.
