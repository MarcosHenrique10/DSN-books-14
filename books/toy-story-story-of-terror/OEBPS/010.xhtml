The crawl space was dark and creepy. The toys heard a noise up ahead.

“That way!” said Woody.

As they walked through cobwebs, Mr. Pricklepants said, “This would be
the part where the characters get separated, then picked off one by—”

He was suddenly snatched into the shadows!

Then Rex disappeared, too!

Buzz, Woody, and Jessie ran away screaming!
