The toys climbed down to find Buzz motionless on the floor. They tickled him. They yelled at him. But it was no use. He wouldn’t move. Suddenly, they heard a car pull into the driveway.

“They’re home!” Mr. Pricklepants yelled.

“Hurry—we’ve got to fix Buzz!” Hamm said.

Rex undid his back panel and stared at the wires. “Which one?” he asked.

“Uh, blue, then red!” said Hamm.

“No! Red, then black!” Mr. Potato Head cried.
