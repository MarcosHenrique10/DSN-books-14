The toys were buzzing with excitement. Bonnie was going to the park with Woody the cowboy, Jessie the cowgirl, and Dolly. The other toy pals were looking forward to a day of play in Bonnie’s room.

Only Jessie was worried. “Keep an eye on Buzz,” she whispered to her friends. “He’s been acting funny. I think he may have a loose wire.”

“No problem-o,” Hamm the piggy bank said.
