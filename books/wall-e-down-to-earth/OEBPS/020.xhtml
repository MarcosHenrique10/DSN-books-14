Unfortunately, WALL•E had been crushed under
 the weight of the machine. He fell to the floor, broken.

“WALL•E?” said EVE, holding him close. But the
 hero robot didn’t answer.

As soon as the Axiom landed on Earth, EVE
 whisked WALL•E back to his home to try to repair
 him. The reject-bots gathered around and
 watched hopefully as she tried to find the
 right parts.
