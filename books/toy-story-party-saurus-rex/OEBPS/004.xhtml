One afternoon, Bonnie’s toys were having
a bubble party in her room.

“You’ll get soap on the floor!” Rex cried
nervously. He ran toward a giant bubble, and
pop! It burst in a spray of soapy water.  

Mr. Potato Head and the other toys were
annoyed. “Party-Pooper Rex!
Party-Pooper Rex!” the toys sang.

Rex was embarrassed. He didn’t
want to be a party pooper.
