Meanwhile, when Rex hadn’t returned, Buzz, Woody, and the rest

of the gang had gone looking for him . . . and gotten soaked!

“You guys missed it!” said Rex proudly as Jessie dried the toys

with a fan. “I was a partysaurus!” He didn’t mention how
frightened he had been.

“You?” scoffed Mr. Potato Head. “I’ll believe it when I see it.”

Just then, a voice came through the window. “Psst! Partysaurus!”
